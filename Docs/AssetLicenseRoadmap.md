# Asset licence additions

## Online account UI — 2026-11-11

The owner confirmed that OGolf's driver, wood, iron and putter club-face images
are cleared for reuse in this public project. Their source is the maintainer's
OGolf repository at revision `41ada2d`, under `assets/images/`.

The unchanged PNGs are in `Content/UI/Clubs/`; each is 1619 × 971 pixels. They are
used by Slate and staged as loose runtime resources. No map, turf, vegetation,
lighting or 3D material asset was modified for this feature.

The QR encoder is Project Nayuki's MIT-licensed C library, version 1.8.0. Its
licence is retained in both source files and listed in
[the plugin notice](../Plugins/OpenGolfOnline/NOTICE.md) and
[third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Rendered validation and its limits are recorded in [Phase status](PhasesStatus.md).
