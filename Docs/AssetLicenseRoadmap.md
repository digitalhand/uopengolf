# Asset licence additions

## Cut-turf rough — 2026-11-20

The range's rough band is now original project art: a 50 mm `Rough` family generated by
`Plugins/OpenGolfSavanna/Scripts/blender/turf_rules.py` (see [50 mm rough](TurfWorkflow.md#50-mm-rough--2026-11-20)).
A public Blender tutorial was used only as a visual reference for blade shape and colour; no assets
or files from it are included. The Poly Haven Grass Medium 01 (CC0) tiles are no longer placed on
the range but stay in the plugin, so the third-party notices are unchanged.

## Online account UI — 2026-11-11

The owner confirmed that OGolf's driver, wood, iron and putter club-face images
are cleared for reuse in this public project. Their source is the maintainer's
OGolf repository at revision `41ada2d`, under `assets/images/`.

The unchanged PNGs are in `Content/UI/Clubs/`; each is 1619 × 971 pixels. They are
used by Slate and staged as loose runtime resources. No map, turf, vegetation,
lighting or 3D material asset was modified for this feature.

The QR encoder is Project Nayuki's MIT-licensed C library, version 1.8.0. Its
licence is retained in both source files and listed in
[the plugin notice](../Plugins/OpenGolfOnline/NOTICE.md) and
[third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Rendered validation and its limits are recorded in [Phase status](PhasesStatus.md).

## Daylight and weather — 2026-11-20

The reusable environment plugin owns the daylight profile, cloud material and density shader. The material references Unreal Engine’s built-in `T_VolumeNoiseShape128` and `T_VolumeNoiseErosion32` volumes for cloud shape and edge detail. The textures come from the engine installation. The owner's photograph served as a visual reference. See [rendered validation](PhasesStatus.md) and [engine asset notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Training target ring — 2026-11-23

The single 3 ft, 6 ft, or 10 ft ring uses the existing project materials `M_ShotTrail` and
`M_TargetRingLine` with the existing ground label. See [rendered checks](PhasesStatus.md#training-game-picker-and-winning-radius--2026-11-23).

## Player avatars — 2026-11-24

The 24 built-in player avatars are Microsoft's Fluent Emoji 3D images (MIT, Copyright (c) Microsoft
Corporation), pinned at commit `1ffb34c752ecf5d402f04cfb4b392c77f57c54bc`. They are imported unchanged as
256 px UI textures in `Plugins/OpenGolfSavanna/Content/UI/Avatars/`, renamed for their golf use (for example
`T_Avatar_Bogey` for the ghost). The MIT licence text must accompany copies; it is in
`Plugins/OpenGolfSavanna/LICENSES/MIT-fluentui-emoji.txt`, with the source and pins in
[the plugin notice](../Plugins/OpenGolfSavanna/NOTICE.md#player-avatars-fluent-emoji-mit) and
[third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). Downloaded originals stay in the art root
(`FluentEmoji/`). No map, turf, vegetation, lighting or 3D material asset was modified.

A signed-in player's own account picture is downloaded at run time from the address their login
service names and is never stored in the repository or on disk.
